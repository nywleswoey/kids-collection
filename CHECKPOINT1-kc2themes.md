## Theme: Prehistoric Sea Monsters
(Ocean reptiles and fish from the dinosaur era. Distinct from the existing "Dinosaurs" theme — land dinosaurs — and "Deep Sea Creatures" — modern abyssal animals.)

**Legendary (2)**
- Megalodon — biggest predatory shark ever, the record-holder
- Mosasaurus — apex sea reptile, among the first prehistoric fossils ever studied (1764)

**Epic (5)**
- Plesiosaurus — the long-necked icon that defined "sea monster"
- Ichthyosaurus — dolphin-shaped reptile, famous early fossil find
- Dunkleosteus — armor-plated giant fish, bone-crushing bite
- Liopleurodon — massive short-necked predator, dramatic and feared
- Elasmosaurus — longest neck of any sea reptile

**Rare (8)**
- Kronosaurus — huge-skulled, powerful-jawed hunter
- Tylosaurus — giant mosasaur, fast coastal predator
- Xiphactinus — giant fanged bony fish
- Helicoprion — shark with a spiral whorl of teeth
- Archelon — biggest sea turtle that ever lived
- Shonisaurus — giant long-snouted ichthyosaur
- Pliosaurus — short-necked, crushing-jawed hunter
- Leedsichthys — enormous gentle filter-feeding fish

**Common (15)**
- Metriorhynchus — crocodile-like, fully ocean-adapted
- Temnodontosaurus — ichthyosaur with huge eyes
- Cryptoclidus — small long-necked plesiosaur
- Platecarpus — common streamlined mosasaur
- Globidens — mosasaur with round shell-crushing teeth
- Henodus — flat, armored turtle-like reptile
- Placodus — shellfish-crushing coastal reptile
- Nothosaurus — amphibious early marine reptile
- Hybodus — common ancient shark
- Dakosaurus — marine-crocodile relative, strong swimmer
- Styxosaurus — classic long-necked plesiosaur
- Simosaurus — small early marine reptile
- Eurhinosaurus — ichthyosaur with an extra-long snout
- Thalassomedon — large long-necked plesiosaur
- Cymbospondylus — early, eel-like ichthyosaur
